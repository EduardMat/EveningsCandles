CANDLES

LUXURY CANDLES

Crafted with care. Glowing with soul. How it started.

It didn’t begin as a business, but as a quiet moment on a winter evening when I lit a candle and felt the stress of the day fade away. That simple sense of calm sparked my curiosity, and I began experimenting—melting wax, blending scents, and learning through many imperfect attempts.
What I discovered was that a candle is more than just a product—it’s an experience. It can bring warmth, comfort, and meaning to everyday moments. As I improved, friends and family began sharing them, turning small creations into something much bigger.
That’s how Evenings Candles was born—built on the idea of slowing down, connecting, and creating simple, beautiful moments. Each candle is made to bring a sense of calm, warmth, and togetherness into your home.

Flowers arrangements

Elegant, handcrafted soy wax flower arrangements that look like real blooms and fill your space with long-lasting fragrance. Eco-friendly, beautiful, and made to never fade—perfect for gifting or treating yourself.

Wax melts
Odorizants

Fill your home with beautiful, long-lasting fragrance using our scented soy wax melts—available in charming shapes like hearts and flowers or classic melt cubes. Made from non-toxic, eco-friendly soy wax, they’re a safe and stylish way to enjoy your favorite scents without compromise.

Candles

Elevate your space with our scented soy candles, beautifully poured into elegant glass or ceramic containers. From minimalist designs to stunning pieces adorned with delicate flowers, candle offers a clean, long-lasting fragrance and a touch of effortless luxury—perfect for cozy moments or thoughtful gifts.

Baby shower

Adorable scented soy wax figurines—featuring sweet bears, lions, and giraffes—perfect for baby showers and special celebrations. Made from non-toxic, eco-friendly soy wax and customizable for a personal touch, they’re a charming, safe, and memorable favor your guests will love.

Home decor

Add a touch of sophistication to your home with our unique, handcrafted décor pieces made from ceramic powder. From elegant candle trays to chic jewelry containers and decorative vases, each item is stylish, distinctive, and designed to stand out beautifully in any space.

Kids colouring items

Spark creativity and fun with our coloring gypsum figurines—featuring princesses, building bricks, and playful robots. Perfect for creative kids, or as a unique party activity, these charming pieces turn imagination into hands-on joy.
