Crafted with care. Glowing with soul. How it started.

Evening Candle began with a simple love for creating beautiful things by hand. What started as a hobby quickly became a passion as I discovered how much joy there is in designing candles that are not only beautiful to look at but also bring warmth and comfort into a home.
Every piece is handcrafted with care, using natural soy wax and thoughtfully chosen details.
Over time, my collection grew beyond candles to include elegant ceramic pieces and carefully curated gift sets, each designed to be both decorative and meaningful.
For me, it's the little details that make something special—a unique shape, a delicate flower, a beautifully crafted ceramic vessel, or a thoughtful gift that becomes a lasting keepsake.
My hope is that every creation brings a sense of warmth, elegance, and joy, whether it's part of your home or a gift for someone you love.
