Safety Sheets

Safety instructions creative plaster figurines

PRODUCT SAFETY INFORMATION Paint Your Own Plaster Figurines Set Product description: Creative arts and crafts painting set Set contents: Plaster figurines (assorted designs) Acrylic paint pots (assorted colours) 1 paintbrush 1. Intended Use This product is a creative arts and crafts activity designed for decorating pre-made plaster figurines using the supplied acrylic paints and paintbrush. Use only for its intended purpose. This product is not a food item or cosmetic product. 2. Safety Warnings WARNING: Adult supervision is recommended during use. Keep out of reach of children when not in use. Do not swallow plaster figurines or acrylic paints. Avoid contact with eyes and mouth. Do not inhale plaster dust. Do not sand, grind or deliberately break the plaster figurines. Broken plaster pieces may have sharp edges and should be handled carefully. Small parts may present a choking hazard. Do not allow children to put figurines or paintbrushes in their mouths. Do not eat or drink while using the paints. Wash hands thoroughly after completing the activity. Use in a well-ventilated area. 3. Safe Handling Instructions Plaster Figurines Handle figurines carefully to prevent breakage. Avoid dropping or throwing the figurines. Do not intentionally scrape, crush or sand the plaster. If a figurine breaks, collect the pieces carefully and dispose of them safely. Keep broken pieces away from young children. Acrylic Paints Use the supplied paints only for decorating the plaster figurines. Avoid contact with eyes, mouth and skin. Keep paint pots closed when not in use. Do not mix paints with other chemicals or cleaning products. Do not use the paints on food, food containers or kitchen utensils. Clean accidental spills promptly. 4. First Aid Information Eye Contact: If paint or plaster dust enters the eyes, rinse carefully with plenty of clean water. Seek medical advice if irritation continues. Skin Contact: Wash the affected area with soap and water. Discontinue use if skin irritation develops and seek medical advice if necessary. Ingestion: Rinse the mouth with water. Do not induce vomiting. Seek medical advice, particularly if plaster or paint has been swallowed. Inhalation: Move to fresh air if plaster dust is inhaled. Seek medical advice if coughing, breathing difficulties or irritation persist. 5. Storage Store in a cool, dry place. Keep paint pots securely closed when not in use. Protect plaster figurines from moisture and physical damage. Keep the complete set away from young children when not in use. 6. Disposal Dispose of unused paints, empty paint pots and broken plaster pieces responsibly, in accordance with local waste disposal requirements. Do not pour large quantities of paint into drains. 7. Important Information This product is intended for craft and decorative use only. Always follow the instructions and safety information supplied with the individual paint products.
